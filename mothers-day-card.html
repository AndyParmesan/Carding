A message for you

With all my love

Happy Mother's Day Para sa aking Nanay

✦

There are three women of this generation whose names echo with the strength of their convictions — Dr. Leni Robredo, Senator Miriam Defensor Santiago, and Ms. Risa Hontiveros — women who have stood at the forefront of justice, who have raised their voices for equality, for truth, and for a country worthy of the people who love it. Their courage has moved mountains and stirred the conscience of a nation.

And yet, for all the quiet awe I hold for them — for all the history they have written in ink and fire — none of them hold the particular kind of strength that you carry, Mom. Yours is a different order of greatness altogether.

Since I was small, you were simply there. Always. Not with fanfare or speeches, but in the steady, irreplaceable way that only a mother knows how to be. I think of those last-minute school supply runs — the ones I would spring on you with the careless confidence of a child who never doubted you would come through. Rightfully annoyed as you were, you went anyway. You always went. Not because it was easy, but because I had asked, and that was reason enough for you.

There are so many such moments woven into the fabric of my life, each one a small proof of a love that shows up without condition. Even in those times I wore an irritated face toward you — you always saw through it. You knew it was never truly meant the way it looked, and you let it pass with a grace I am still learning to match. You forgave before I even thought to ask.

I will carry gratitude for you for the rest of my life. I count it among the greatest fortunes I have ever been given — that of all the mothers in this world, God saw fit to give me you. Every day, I thank Him for that quiet miracle.

Forever and always, Your Firstborn, AJ
